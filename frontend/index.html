How it works

Three steps, then just study

  1. 1

    Add an exam

    Enter the date, the chapters and how many minutes you can study each day.

  2. 2

    Follow the daily plan

    Softnote splits chapters into sessions. Harder chapters get more time.

  3. 3

    Tick off what you finish

    Press Done after each session. Missed one? It moves to your next free day.
