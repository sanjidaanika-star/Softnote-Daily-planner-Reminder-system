Get started in 3 steps

Softnote does the planning. You just study and tick things off.

  1. 1
    Add an exam

    Enter the exam date, the chapters, and how many minutes you can study each day.

  2. 2
    Follow today’s sessions

    Softnote splits the chapters into daily sessions. Harder chapters get more time.

  3. 3
    Tick off what you finish

    Press Done after each session. Missed one? It moves to your free days automatically.
